# Initial government assistance program information catalog

Verified 2026-10-26 (Hawaiʻi time). These are source-backed discovery records, not a completed eligibility engine. A source may be current while a linked numerical rule remains unverified. Unknown fields below must stay unknown until checked. Application route availability does not establish approval, open capacity or individual eligibility.

## Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)

**Agency / classification:** USDA; Hawaiʻi DHS BESSD

**Funding / administration:** Federal, state administered

**Purpose and benefits:** Food purchasing benefits through EBT.

**Intended population:** Low-income food-purchase/preparation units, with age/disability exceptions.

**Age, income, household, residency and disability rules:** Current DHS chart has 130%/200% gross and 100% net guideline columns, household-size indexed, effective 10/1/2026. BBCE and elderly/disabled rules differ; apply current deductions, resource and status requirements. Hawaiʻi amounts differ from contiguous states.

**Official eligibility:** [Agency documentation](https://humanservices.hawaii.gov/bessd/snap./). **Application/referral:** [Official route](https://pais.dhs.hawaii.gov/).

**Service area:** Statewide

**Local locations:** DHS processing centers: https://humanservices.hawaii.gov/processing-centers-and-first-to-work-units/

**Rule effective date / version:** DHS chart 10/1/2026; current categorical/work-rule details require their own effective versions.

**Application/enrollment status:** Applications linked; case approval not implied.

**Public administrative statistics:** DHS monthly participation links and annual databook; no 2022 SLD administrative series verified.

**Still needed before deterministic eligibility matching:** Encode assistance-unit rules, allowable deductions, BBCE exceptions and all current federal/state changes before any eligibility result.

**Last verified:** 2026-10-26 (Hawaiʻi time).

## Supplemental Security Income (SSI)

**Agency / classification:** Social Security Administration

**Funding / administration:** Federal

**Purpose and benefits:** Means-tested cash support.

**Intended population:** People 65+, or blind/disabled adults and eligible children.

**Age, income, household, residency and disability rules:** Limited countable income/resources; individual/couple unit, deeming/exclusions; U.S. residence and citizenship/qualified-status rules. 2026 general resource limits $2,000/$3,000; payment maximum is not an income cutoff.

**Official eligibility:** [Agency documentation](https://www.ssa.gov/ssi/text-eligibility-ussi.htm). **Application/referral:** [Official route](https://www.ssa.gov/ssi).

**Service area:** U.S. including Hawaiʻi

**Local locations:** SSA official locator from application site.

**Rule effective date / version:** 2026 guidance; preserve announced 2027 rules separately from rules effective now.

**Application/enrollment status:** Application/appointment routes available; not a benefit guarantee.

**Public administrative statistics:** SSA state/county SSI publications should be selected/versioned; published OASDI congressional counts are a different population. No SLD file verified.

**Still needed before deterministic eligibility matching:** Full countable-income, resource exclusions, child/parent/spouse deeming and status rules; exact selected SSI participation file.

**Last verified:** 2026-10-26 (Hawaiʻi time).

**Rule effective date / version:** 2026 packet verified; currentFY2027 income chart not verified as ofOct26.

**Application/enrollment status:** EC annualJune1–30 window is past; ECI accepts year-round subject to limited monthly approvals.

**Public administrative statistics:** Federal/state program summaries may provide aggregate recipients; no SLD series verified.

**Still needed before deterministic eligibility matching:** FY 2027 chart, current agency contacts and availability; do not silently carry2026 amounts into a new program year.
